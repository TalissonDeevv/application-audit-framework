# KISS Principle Module

## Overview

Evaluates whether the project follows the Keep It Simple, Stupid (KISS) principle.

---

## Objective

Identify unnecessary complexity that reduces readability and maintainability.

---

## What to Inspect

Inspect:

- Over-engineering
- Excessive abstractions
- Unnecessary layers
- Complex algorithms
- Premature optimization
- Unnecessary patterns

---

## Common Findings

- Over-engineered solutions
- Unnecessary abstractions
- Excessive design patterns
- Complex implementations
- Premature optimization

---

## Severity

High when complexity significantly impacts maintainability.

Medium for unnecessary complexity.

Low for simplification opportunities.

---

## Expected Evidence

- Deep inheritance
- Multiple abstraction layers
- Unnecessary interfaces
- Complex control flow

---

## Recommendations

Prefer simple solutions.

Remove unnecessary abstractions.

Reduce complexity.

Avoid premature optimization.

Favor readability.
