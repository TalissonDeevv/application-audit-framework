# Disaster Recovery Module

## Overview

Evaluates disaster recovery planning and operational resilience.

Disaster recovery ensures that critical services can be restored after major failures.

---

## Objective

Identify weaknesses in recovery planning and business continuity.

---

## What to Inspect

Inspect:

- Disaster recovery plan
- Recovery procedures
- Recovery testing
- Failover mechanisms
- Recovery objectives
- Infrastructure redundancy
- Incident documentation

---

## Common Findings

- No disaster recovery plan
- Untested recovery procedures
- Missing redundancy
- Undefined recovery objectives
- Manual recovery processes
- Single points of failure

---

## Operational Impact

Possible impacts:

- Extended downtime
- Business interruption
- Financial losses
- Reputation damage

---

## Severity

Critical:

No recovery strategy exists.

High:

Recovery procedures are incomplete.

Medium:

Recovery process needs improvement.

Low:

Documentation improvements.

---

## Expected Evidence

- Recovery documentation
- Infrastructure configuration
- Backup procedures
- Failover configuration
- Operational documentation

---

## Recommendations

Create a disaster recovery plan.

Test recovery procedures regularly.

Implement infrastructure redundancy.

Define recovery objectives.

Document recovery workflows.

---
