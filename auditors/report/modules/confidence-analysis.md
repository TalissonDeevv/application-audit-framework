# Confidence Analysis Module

## Overview

Evaluates the confidence level of all findings.

Confidence represents how certain the AI Audit Framework is about each reported issue.

---

## Objective

Measure the reliability of generated findings.

---

## Responsibilities

The module must:

- Calculate confidence distribution.
- Explain confidence levels.
- Identify findings requiring manual review.
- Highlight highly reliable findings.

---

## Confidence Levels

The report should include:

- Very High
- High
- Medium
- Low

---

## Metrics

Calculate:

- Average confidence
- Distribution by confidence
- Findings requiring validation

---

## Interpretation

Explain:

- Overall confidence
- Reliability of the audit
- Recommended manual verification when necessary

---

## Validation

The analysis must:

- Preserve confidence assigned by auditors.
- Never increase confidence arbitrarily.
- Be evidence-based.

---

## Output Example

Confidence Distribution

Very High: 60%

High: 30%

Medium: 8%

Low: 2%

Overall Confidence:

High

Most findings are strongly supported by available evidence.
