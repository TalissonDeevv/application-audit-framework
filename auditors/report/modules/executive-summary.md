# Executive Summary Module

## Overview

Generates a high-level summary of the audit for executives, managers and stakeholders.

This section should communicate the overall health of the project without excessive technical details.

---

## Objective

Provide a concise overview of the audit results.

---

## Include

- Overall project health
- Total findings
- Critical risks
- Overall score
- Main strengths
- Main weaknesses
- Recommended priorities

---

## Audience

Designed for:

- CTOs
- CEOs
- Engineering Managers
- Product Owners
- Clients

---

## Writing Style

The summary should be:

- Short
- Objective
- Business-oriented
- Easy to understand

---

## Validation

The Executive Summary must:

- Reflect the complete audit.
- Mention critical issues.
- Avoid implementation details.
- Be understandable by non-developers.

---

## Output Example

Executive Summary

Overall Score: 82/100

Critical Findings: 2

High Findings: 6

Medium Findings: 14

Low Findings: 8

The project demonstrates good architectural quality but contains security and infrastructure issues that should be addressed before production deployment.
