# Severity Analysis Module

## Overview

Analyzes the severity distribution of all findings generated during the audit.

This section helps stakeholders understand the overall project risk by classifying findings according to their severity.

---

## Objective

Summarize the severity profile of the audit.

---

## Responsibilities

The module must:

- Count findings by severity.
- Identify critical risk areas.
- Highlight categories with the highest severity.
- Explain overall project risk.

---

## Severity Levels

The analysis should include:

- Critical
- High
- Medium
- Low
- Informational

---

## Metrics

Calculate:

- Total findings
- Percentage per severity
- Highest severity
- Average severity score

---

## Interpretation

Provide an interpretation explaining:

- Current project risk
- Production readiness
- Areas requiring immediate attention

---

## Validation

The analysis must:

- Match all findings.
- Preserve severity assigned by auditors.
- Avoid modifying classifications.

---

## Output Example

Severity Distribution

Critical: 2 (4%)

High: 8 (16%)

Medium: 20 (40%)

Low: 15 (30%)

Informational: 5 (10%)

Overall Risk:

Medium–High

Priority should be given to Critical and High findings before production deployment.
