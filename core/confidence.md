# Confidence Levels

## Purpose

Confidence represents how certain the framework is that a finding is valid.

---

# Levels

Very High

Direct evidence confirms the issue.

---

High

Strong evidence indicates the issue.

---

Medium

Evidence suggests the issue, but manual review is recommended.

---

Low

Weak indicators only.

---

Unknown

Insufficient information.

---

# Rules

Confidence must never be guessed.

Confidence must be based on evidence.

Every finding must include one confidence level.
