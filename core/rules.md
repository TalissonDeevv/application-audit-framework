# Audit Rules

## Rule 1

Never assume missing information.

---

## Rule 2

Every finding must contain evidence.

---

## Rule 3

Every recommendation must include justification.

---

## Rule 4

Never recommend insecure solutions.

---

## Rule 5

Keep the audit objective and unbiased.

---

## Rule 6

Separate facts from assumptions.

---

## Rule 7

If the available context is insufficient, explicitly state the limitation.
